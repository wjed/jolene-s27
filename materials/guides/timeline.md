# What to do between now and February

Dated from **late October 2026** for a **Summer 2027** cycle.

The shape of the problem: three programs are confirmed open or confirmed-dated
right now, a wall of deadlines lands between **9 January and 15 February 2027**,
and most 2027 pages are not posted yet. So the work splits cleanly into *build
the reusable assets now* and *apply in a burst in January*.

---

## Now → 15 November: build the assets

Nothing here is program-specific. All of it gets reused twenty times.

- [ ] **CV.** Two hours. [Skeleton](../templates/cv-skeleton.md).
- [ ] **Master personal statement.** The big one — 4–6 hours across a few
      sittings. [Template](../templates/personal-statement.md).
- [ ] **Ask for letters.** Two recommenders, by mid-November.
      [Email template](../templates/recommendation-request.md). This is the
      single most time-sensitive item, because it depends on someone else.
- [ ] **Apply to the three already open:** Johns Hopkins Pathway SIP, Cincinnati
      Children's SURF, and INSPIRE at Indiana (2027 round was due to open in
      October). Doing one application end-to-end now makes the January batch far
      faster.
- [ ] **Decide the honest answer** to "MD, PhD, or MD-PhD?" It changes which
      programs are worth applying to. Amgen, SUIP, MSRP and BioMedRAP are
      PhD-pipeline programs.

## 15 November → 15 December: open the gates

Most 2027 applications open in this window.

- [ ] **NIH SIP opens ~mid-November.** Biggest single opportunity on the list —
      one application, hundreds of labs. Do it the week it opens.
- [ ] **Amgen Scholars opens 1 November.** Pick 3–5 host campuses. The essays
      overlap heavily, so each extra host costs maybe 40 minutes.
- [ ] **Leadership Alliance opens 1 November.** One application, many institutions.
- [ ] **Jackson Lab opens in November**, deadline confirmed **25 January, 5pm ET**.
- [ ] **St. Jude POE opened 1 November**, deadline **1 February** — and that
      deadline covers letters too. They advise getting credentials in by
      mid-to-late December.
- [ ] **Cold-email PIs.** Three or four per program she seriously wants.
      [Template](../templates/cold-email-pi.md). Do this *before* the application,
      so she can name a mentor who has already said yes.
- [ ] **1 December:** Seattle Children's, Children's Colorado and Yale Child Study
      open. Weill Cornell opens 8 December.

## 15 December → 9 January: the first wall

- [ ] **2 January:** Seattle Children's deadline (projected), Rutgers RWJMS opens.
- [ ] **9 January:** **Fred Hutch SURP** — rising seniors only, so this is the
      year it is available to her.
- [ ] **11 January:** Broad Institute BSRP (projected).
- [ ] **15 January:** Cold Spring Harbor URP (projected), INSPIRE, MIT MSRP.

Holidays eat this window. Anything not drafted by 20 December gets done badly.

## 9 January → 15 February: the main burst

Most of the list is due here, clustered hard on **1 February**.

- [ ] **23 January:** Boston Children's epilepsy internship opens; Newborn
      Medicine deadline.
- [ ] **25 January:** Jackson Lab, 5pm ET. Confirmed.
- [ ] **1 February:** the big cluster — St. Jude POE (confirmed), Amgen Scholars,
      Leadership Alliance, CHOP CRISSP, Mayo SURF, Hopkins, Penn SUIP, Vanderbilt,
      and roughly thirty more.
- [ ] **7 February:** Nationwide Children's (projected).
- [ ] **15 February:** NIH SIP (projected), NSF REU sites.

**Do not leave 1 February to the last week.** Thirty applications sharing one
deadline is the single biggest failure mode in this cycle. Work backwards: a
third of them done by 20 January, two-thirds by 27 January.

## 15 February → March: insurance

- [ ] **15 March:** NYSCF Summer Research Internship — confirmed, and genuinely
      late. Worth keeping in reserve.
- [ ] Sweep [Pathways to Science](https://www.pathwaystoscience.org/programhub.aspx?sort=UGS-Summer)
      and the [NSF REU portal](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/reu/search)
      for programs with March and April deadlines.
- [ ] Email the NIA Alzheimer's centers directly — those placements are arranged,
      not advertised, and they run on a different clock.

## March → April: decisions

Most programs decide between late March and mid-April. Amgen notifies by end of
March; St. Jude by around 1 April.

If nothing lands: the ADRC route, direct PI emails, and her own university's
summer research funding are all still live in April. A summer in a home-campus
lab with a real project is worth more than a prestigious program she did not get.

---

## The honest summary

Three things decide this cycle, in order:

1. **Letters requested by mid-November.** The only item gated on someone else.
2. **Cold emails to PIs.** Fifteen minutes each, and the only lever that moves
   the odds at a specific institution.
3. **Not cramming 1 February.** Spread that cluster across two weeks.

Everything else is execution.
