# Research CV: skeleton

One page. Two only if there is genuinely a second page of content, which at this
stage there usually is not.

A research CV is not a job resume. Drop the objective statement, drop "Microsoft
Office", and lead with education and research.

Wherever the skeleton says `[TAB]`, the part after it sits flush against the
right margin. How to do that in Google Docs or Word, without spaces, is the
first rule below the skeleton.

## Rules that matter

- **Do not fake the right-hand column with spaces.** The skeleton marks it with
  [TAB] because spaces do not survive the trip into a word processor: in Google
  Docs or Word every run of spaces lands somewhere different and the right edge
  comes out ragged. Use a right-aligned tab stop. In Google Docs, click the
  ruler at the right margin and add a right tab stop there. In Word, open the
  Paragraph settings, choose Tabs, and set a right-aligned tab at the right
  margin. Then press Tab once wherever the skeleton says [TAB]. A two-column
  table with the borders turned off works too. Both survive editing and the PDF
  export.
- **Use the bullet button** for the · lines instead of typing the dots, so a
  bullet that wraps onto a second line stays indented.
- **Reverse chronological** within every section.
- **No pronouns.** "Performed immunostaining," not "I performed immunostaining."
- **Verbs first.** Not "Was responsible for maintaining cell cultures" but
  "Maintained cell cultures."
- **Be specific about skills.** "Proficient in laboratory techniques" says
  nothing. "Mouse colony management, genotyping by PCR" says a lot.
- **Quantify where it is honest.** "Enrolled 40 patients," "analyzed 12 months of
  registry data."
- **Certifications near the top.** CITI human subjects and Good Clinical
  Practice training is something most undergraduate applicants do not have, so
  do not bury it under skills.
- **One page, 10-11pt, generous margins.** A cramped two-pager reads worse than a
  clean one-pager.
- **Export to PDF**, named `Lastname-Firstname-CV.pdf`. Never send a .docx.

## If the research section is thin

Common, and not disqualifying. Seattle Children's says prior formal research
experience is not required and prioritizes applicants seeking their first formal
research internship, and plenty of others take students with no bench experience
at all.

What to do: promote a serious course project into the research section with its
real question and method. List coursework that signals readiness (statistics,
biochem, a lab-heavy course). Put technical skills higher so they are seen early.
Do not pad with high-school material.
