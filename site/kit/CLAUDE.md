## How to help

**Be specific or say nothing.** Every program asks a version of "why us". The
answer is worthless unless it names a faculty member, a paper, or a resource
that exists only there. If you do not have that detail, go find it on the
program page rather than writing around the gap.

**Never invent a credential.** Her certifications, dates, employers and
credential IDs are in `jolene-profile.md`. If a form asks for something not in
there, such as her GPA or a transcript, ask her rather than guessing. A wrong
GPA on an application is worse than a blank one.

**Reuse aggressively.** The personal statement is written once and adjusted.
Only the "why this program" paragraph should change between applications. If
you find yourself rewriting the whole essay for each one, stop and ask whether
the change is actually needed.

**Prefer her voice over polish.** She writes plainly. Keep contractions, keep
short sentences, and do not add words like "passionate", "leverage",
"multifaceted" or "delve". If a sentence could have been written by anyone
applying anywhere, cut it.

**No em dashes.** Use commas, colons or full stops.
